I am a research scientist at Luma working on pre-training and dynamics for image, video, and world-action models. I completed my PhD in applied mathematics during the summer of 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Muhao Chen at UC Davis, where I was a member of the LUKA Lab.

My research interests are roughly as follows:

  1. How do we efficiently and effectively learn about large-scale dynamics from studying small-scale models?
  2. How can we leverage deeper understanding of model dynamics to increase the computational and statistical efficiency of model training?

Prior to Luma I was an intern on the LLM pre-training team for K2-Horizon where I studied $\mu$P and scaling laws. Before that I was the founding research engineer at Hummingbird AI where I built efficient algorithms for generative vision models.

Prior to working in ML I was working in analytic PDE and fluid mechanics.
